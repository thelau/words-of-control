# Reference words — draft for the artist to edit

The norm every visitor's words are measured against: what "matters" (far from these), ±σ (in their spread), the
nearest words (among these), the ending's line (the words placed among them). Proposed stance: **ordinary language** —
the words people use every day, the feelings they name, the short things they say to each other — so that "unusual"
means departing from how people ordinarily speak — and **what people type into a piece like this**: talking to the
machine, confessions, questions, anger, images, the world, names, daily life, children, sayings and songs, other
languages, non-words and long sentences (the second ~600, from 2026-10-04, chosen against a held-out set of simulated
visitors: docs/visitor-simulation.md, scored by scripts/simulate.ts). ~1,000 entries, recorded once (one Jev call
each), never visitors' words. Other languages: only words English has no word for — a plain translation only
echoes the visitor's own word back as its "nearest" (love → amour); words typed in another language are still
measured, and land near their meaning. Cut, add, or replace freely.
