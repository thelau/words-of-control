# Reference words — draft for the artist to edit

The norm every visitor's words are measured against: what "matters" (far from these), ±σ (in their spread), the
nearest words (among these), the ending's line (the words placed among them). Proposed stance: **ordinary language** —
the words people use every day, the feelings they name, the short things they say to each other — so that "unusual"
means departing from how people ordinarily speak. ~400 entries (words, and everyday sentences so that sentences are measured against sentences too), recorded once (one Jev call each), never visitors'
words. Cut, add, or replace freely; one entry per line within each group.
