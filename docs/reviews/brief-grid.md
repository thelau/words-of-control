# Review brief — Words of Control, "the grid" (2026-09-27, round 9: step-27+)

You are reviewing a new media art installation in progress by Laurent Thevenet. Read `docs/GRID.md` first (the
current direction and engine). Frames are in `docs/captures/review/` (local): for each word a contact sheet
`sheet-<word>.png` and the individual frames `<word>-NN-<moment>.png` (2880 × 1800, a 16" laptop screen), in time
order: the fill, the grid filled, the cells marked, cleared, then steps (tiles, the 3D spaces — `-full` = the whole
frame —, the last step "stand"), black, and the room after. Words: war, love, spoon, nothing, mother, fuck,
"i miss you", "to be or not to be". Loudness and spectrograms: `docs/captures/audio/spectrum-<word>.png`.

## What a visitor lives through (about 14–18 s per words)

- **Room:** black; "YOUR WORDS" faint above a cursor; one thin white line under it — the drone's own waveform, read
  live from the sound — swelling as they type. A low drone (the harmonics of D, slowly beating).
- **Enter:** one call to Jev, an AI that only *judges* the words (45 questions: valence, arousal, material, colour,
  violence, ambiguity, concreteness…). Nothing shown or heard is AI-generated; everything is authored code driven by
  the answers.
- **Fill (~2 s):** a 9 × 5 grid; each answer lands in its cell — its name (tracked capitals), its value, a small live
  figure (a sine whose frequency is the value, bars for a choice's options, dots for a yes/no; noise where Jev is
  unsure). Each lands with a 25 ms test tone, panned where the cell is.
- **Mark / clear (~2.5 s):** the 4–7 answers that set the words apart from 61 reference words (chosen by the artist),
  among those Jev is sure of, take the words' own colours (Jev's colour answer); their labels grow. The rest go out,
  staccato, a dry click each. Held ~1 s.
- **Steps (~8–10 s):** cut to the marked cells merged in the centre (a 7 × 3 stage), then a 4/4 at a tempo from the
  words (92–150 bpm): one 3D space per bar, chosen by the data — cloud (where the words sit among the reference words,
  axes = the answers that matter), network (joined to its nearest words, named), ridges (how far it stands out) or
  table (all 45 answers × the reference words), terrain or contour map (the ground it stands on), globe (all answers)
  in the last bar — the camera cutting on each beat, labels on the data (axes, the words, nearest words). The 24 cells
  around repeat the space drawn other ways (angles, scan series, sections, close-ups). Some downbeats fill the frame.
  Sound: one crescendo — two voices of the marked answers on fixed euclidean patterns (lengths K and K+1, phasing),
  a growing ping-pong delay, noise ticks, sub pulses; the drone silent.
  **Mood:** negative (war, fuck) — camera cuts on 8ths/16ths, a safe strobe (≤ 2 pale-grey frames a second), sound by
  subtraction (a click grid, test-tone blips, silence on the strobe frames); neutral (spoon, nothing) — clinical
  test tones, long-lens technical drawings; positive (love, mother) — softer larger points, longer notes, a chord.
- **Stand (~3 s):** the words large; the answer that sets them most apart ("VIOLENCE 0.98"); the reference words as
  dots along that answer 0–1, the words among them (rank seen, not written); its nearest words; "61 REFERENCE WORDS,
  CHOSEN BY THE ARTIST". A short pure tone.
- **Black:** cut; the hall reverb blooms once; the room returns.

Captions are labels only, in tracked capitals; the visitor's words always as typed. Visitors' words are never
stored. Earlier reviews: docs/reviews/round8-*.md (what changed since: the ending, one space per bar chosen by data,
the lattice replaced by the real table, the music rebuilt, the strobe made safe, capitals).
