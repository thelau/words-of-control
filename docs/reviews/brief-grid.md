# Review brief — Words of Control, "the grid" (2026-09-27, step-25+)

You are reviewing a new media art installation in progress by Laurent Thevenet. Read `docs/GRID.md` first (the
current direction and engine). Frames are in `docs/captures/review/` (local): for each word a contact sheet
`sheet-<word>.png` and the individual frames `<word>-NN-<moment>.png` (2880 × 1800, a 16" laptop screen), in time
order: the fill, the grid filled, the cells marked, cleared, then steps (tiles, the 3D spaces — `-full` = the whole
frame —, the last step "stand"), black, and the room after. Words: war, love, spoon, nothing, mother, fuck,
"i miss you", "to be or not to be". Loudness and spectrograms: `docs/captures/audio/spectrum-<word>.png`.

## What a visitor lives through (about 12–16 s per word)

- **Room:** black; a cursor; one thin white line under it — the drone's own waveform, read live from the sound —
  swelling as they type. A low drone (the harmonics of D, slowly beating).
- **Enter:** one call to Jev, an AI that only *judges* the words (45 questions: valence, arousal, material, colour,
  violence, ambiguity…). Nothing shown or heard is AI-generated; everything is authored code driven by the answers.
- **Fill (~2 s):** a 9 × 5 grid; each answer lands in its cell — its name, its value, a small live figure (a sine, bars,
  dots; noise where Jev is unsure). Each lands with a 25 ms test tone (Ikeda-like), panned where the cell is.
- **Mark / clear (~1.5 s):** the 4–7 answers that set the word apart from 61 reference words take the word's own colours
  (Jev's colour answer: war red, ocean blue, spoon grey/yellow); a held pure tone each. The rest go out, staccato, a dry
  click each.
- **Steps (~7–9 s):** cut to the marked cells merged in the centre, then on every beat of a 4/4 (92–150 bpm, from the
  word) a different 3D space of the data — point clouds, networks, terrain, contour map, globe, lattice, ridge lines,
  scatter planes — the word placed among the reference words, labelled. The 24 cells around repeat the space as angles,
  scan series, sections, close-ups. The last bar switches twice per beat; some downbeats fill the whole frame.
  Sound: a modular sequencer (after Caterina Barbieri): two arpeggios of the key answers, of lengths K and K+1, drifting
  in and out of phase, through a wavefolder and a filter; euclidean noise ticks and sub pulses.
  **Mood:** negative words (war, fuck) — faster camera cutting on 16ths, white strobe frames, sound of clicks in 32nd
  rolls, glitch bursts, high sine stabs; neutral (spoon, nothing) — clinical test tones, long-lens technical drawings;
  positive (love, mother) — softer larger points, longer notes, a quiet chord.
- **Stand (~2.4 s):** "violence 0.98 · above 61 of 61 words" — the answer that sets the word most apart, and its rank.
  A short pure tone.
- **Black:** cut; the hall reverb blooms once; the room returns.

Captions are labels only (they pass too fast for sentences). Visitors' words are never stored.

## Your task

Look at the frames (use your image reading on the sheets and several full frames), read GRID.md, and review as your
role. Be concrete and unsparing: is it **interesting, entertaining, impressive — and not shallow**? What would you
remember? What is weak, generic, "screensaver", "data-viz cliché", or too derivative of Ikeda? What would make it
stronger — specific, buildable changes, ranked. Keep it under ~700 words. Write your review to the file named in your
instructions, then reply with a 5-line summary.
