# Review brief — Words of Control, "the grid" (2026-09-28, round 11: step-31+)

You are reviewing a new media art installation in progress by Laurent Thevenet. Read `docs/GRID.md` first (the
current direction and engine). Frames are in `docs/captures/review/` (local): for each word a contact sheet
`sheet-<word>.png` and the individual frames `<word>-NN-<moment>.png` (2880 × 1800, a 16" laptop screen), in time
order: the fill, the grid filled, the cells marked, cleared, then steps (tiles, the 3D spaces — `-full` = the whole
frame —, the last step "stand"), black, and the room after. Words: war, love, spoon, mother, "i miss you",
"my father never said he was proud of me", "the oceans are vast and beautiful", nothing. **One visitor typing six
words in a row** (the marked grid, the merged cells, each bar's space, the ending — one row per words):
`docs/captures/review/sequence.png`. Loudness and spectrograms: `docs/captures/audio/spectrum-<word>.png`.

## What a visitor lives through (about 14–18 s per words)

- **Room:** black; "YOUR WORDS" faint above a cursor; one thin white line under it — the drone's own waveform, read
  live from the sound — swelling as they type. A low drone (the harmonics of D, slowly beating).
- **Enter:** one call to Jev, an AI that only *judges* the words (45 questions: valence, arousal, material, colour,
  violence, ambiguity, concreteness…). Nothing shown or heard is AI-generated; everything is authored code driven by
  the answers.
- **Fill (~2 s):** a 9 × 5 grid; each answer lands in its cell — its name (tracked capitals), its value, and a strip:
  the 61 reference words as ticks along 0–1 (as tall as the words that gave that value), the word's own value a bright
  line (noise where Jev is unsure). Each lands with a 25 ms test tone, panned where the cell is.
- **Mark / clear (~2.5 s):** the 4–7 answers that set the words apart from the reference words, among those Jev is
  sure of, take the words' own colours (Jev's colour answer; an orange-red accent if white/grey/black); their labels
  grow. The rest go out, staccato, a dry click each. Held ~1 s.
- **Steps (~8–10 s):** cut to the marked cells merged in the centre (a 7 × 3 stage), each tile with its strip and
  ±σ (how far it stands out); then a 4/4 at a tempo from the words (92–150 bpm): one 3D space per bar, chosen by the
  data — cloud (one point a word, stems to the floor; axes = the answers that matter), network (laid out by likeness
  on all 45 answers, joined to the nearest words, named), ridges or the whole table (45 answers × the reference
  words), terrain or contour map, then the ending's answer as a dot plot (axis) in the last bar — the camera cutting
  on each beat, labels on the data. The 24 cells around are small multiples: the reference words in the space of each
  three of the answers that matter most, labelled, the word a cross in its colour, each turning and cutting on the
  beat. Header: "45 ANSWERS → 7 THAT MATTER → WHERE IT STANDS". Sound: one crescendo — two voices of the marked answers
  (pitch from which answer and how far it stands out) on fixed euclidean patterns (lengths K and K+1, phasing), a
  growing ping-pong delay, noise ticks, sub pulses; the drone silent.
  **Mood:** negative (war, fuck) — camera cuts on 8ths/16ths, a safe strobe (≤ 2 pale-grey frames a second), sound by
  subtraction (a click grid, test-tone blips, silence on the strobe frames); neutral (spoon, nothing) — clinical
  test tones, long-lens technical drawings; positive (love, mother) — softer larger points, longer notes, a chord.
- **Stand (≥ 6 s):** the words large; the answer that sets them most apart ("VIOLENCE 0.98"); the reference words as
  a stacked dot plot along it 0–1, the words among them; the nearest on this answer with lines to their dots; the
  nearest on all 45 answers; "45 QUESTIONS ANSWERED BY AN AI (JEV), SURE TO 0.99 · 61 REFERENCE WORDS, CHOSEN BY THE
  ARTIST". A short pure tone; the drone returns under it.
- **Black:** cut; the hall reverb blooms once; the room returns.

Captions are labels only, in tracked capitals; the visitor's words always as typed. Visitors' words are never
stored. Known and open: the 61 reference words are still the artist's test list (it includes asdfgh, qwerty,
bitcoin) — the artist will choose a real set. Earlier reviews: docs/reviews/round8-*.md, round9-*.md.

Since round 10: the reference set is now 400 entries of ordinary language (everyday things, people, places, feelings,
ideas, actions, short things people say, everyday sentences) recorded with Jev; "what matters" is judged by rarity
among them; the ending says the answer in plain words ("COMPLETELY SINCERE", "AWE", "OVERWHELMING") with the nearest
words and phrases ("he never came back", "she didn't call back"); the ring round the stage is played by the sequencer
(each note lights the next cell — one voice clockwise, the other anticlockwise); the whole lasts ~25–30 s.

**The artist's concern now: it may be too boring in repetition** — the same structure, order and look for every
words, and a visitor (or someone watching others) sees many in a row. Judge this first: what repeats that should
not, what should stay constant (a ritual) and what should vary with the words, and how.
