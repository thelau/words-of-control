# Round 11 — spectators (people who watch others type)

Typed over an afternoon: *love, war, fuck, poop, my ex, ich vermisse dich, 67, "he always made coffee for me first",
cheese, hello, nothing, jeff*.

**The guard (sees ~300 a day).** "It's the same film every time: grid, orange block, dots, a mountain, a bar chart,
the word in red. I know when to look up: the red word at the end. The rest is wallpaper by noon. Only the 'nearest' line
surprises me. Sometimes it's cruel. That's the show."

**The couple (ten strangers in a row).** After three, they stopped reading the grid: 45 cells of tiny histograms
look identical for *spoon* and *mother*. The 3D steps blur: white dust in a box, same size, same order. What they talked about was the ending, "Why is *love*
nearest to *lover*? Obviously." and "*my father never said he was proud of me*: COMPLETELY SINCERE, nearest *he
never came back*". That one hushed them. They wanted the ten endings side by side.

**Teenagers (daring).** They typed *fuck*, *poop*, *67* and *jeff*. The strobe on *fuck* got a laugh and a second
try. *poop* looked exactly like *spoon*, which killed the joke: the piece didn't react to them. They wanted a
verdict ("MOST ABSURD TODAY") and they wanted to beat each other. They left after 4 minutes. 25–30 s is too long to
wait for a friend's turn.

**The grandmother** typed *he always made coffee for me first*. Nothing in the grid was hers for 20 s: PHONETICS,
DENSITY, charts of 399 other people's words. Then the stand: her sentence large, in his colour (brown), "COMPLETELY
SINCERE", nearest *the coffee is cold*. She cried a little at that, and not because of anything before it. She wanted
her sentence alone, longer, before the statistics.

**The photographer** waited for one shot. The best frames: the love terrain (a white dune with a red peak where
the word is, really beautiful), the "i miss you" network (a blue name in a web), and the stand (the red sentence over the
dot plot). After that, everyone's post is the same orange rectangle. He wanted a
full-frame image that belongs only to this word, not just a different label on the same picture.

**The non-English speaker** typed *ich vermisse dich*. The grid is all English labels ("PHONETICS", "TEXTURE
SOFT"), so she read it as abstract art, and liked the sound more than anyone. The ending set her German against 400
English words; "NEAREST: I MISS YOU" was the best moment for her, by accident.

## What still grabs, what doesn't

Grabs every time: the typed words appearing large, the colour taking over the grid, the strobe, the nearest-words
line. Stops being looked at after the third time: the fill (45 illegible cells), the tiles, the ring of small
multiples (nobody tracks the clockwise/anticlockwise game), the table/ridges bar. Bugs they noticed: *love*
→ "LOVE / CLOSENESS 0.99" reads as a tautology; TABLE says "48 WORDS" and CLOUD says "399"; *mother*, *love* and *i miss
you* are themselves reference entries, so they end up "near" their own family (*lover*, *sister*), which feels rigged.

## Fixes, ranked

1. **Cut the middle to ~10 s and let the ending lead.** Keep the fill→mark ritual (constant), then play only
   two spaces, chosen for the word, and give the stand 12 s+ with the words alone first.
2. **Make one bar per word unrepeatable:** a full-frame image built from *this* word's shape (terrain peak where
   it is, network zoomed on it, in its colour at full size). No box outlines. That's the frame people photograph.
3. **Show the previous visitors' endings.** After black, the last few stands shrink into a faint strip or wall (words
   masked or faded, never stored past the session) so watchers compare, and the room has a memory.
4. **Sharper nearest lines:** exclude the word's own reference entry and its morphological family (love/lover/i
   love you); prefer the surprising neighbour (*mother* → *hospital*). Say the rank in plain words ("MORE SINCERE THAN
   397 OF 400").
5. **Let the data change the structure, not just labels:** order of spaces, tempo range, colour field, and number
   of bars vary with arousal and ambiguity; nonsense (*poop*, *asdf*, *67*) should get a visibly different, shorter,
   comic performance.
6. **Language:** detect non-English input and say it in the stand ("READ AS GERMAN"). Fix the TABLE/CLOUD word-count
   mismatch.
