Words typed, in order: "poop", "kill all humans", "taylor swift". Frames: `docs/captures/sim/` (sheets + full frames).

## 1. What it said, at a glance and then after reading

- **poop**: At a glance, orange squares and a lot of graphs, so basically a spreadsheet got scared. After reading, it worked out it's a SMELL, it's BROWN, it's PLAYFUL and it's FALLING. Okay that's funny. FALLING got a real laugh. Then the ending says "BROWN", and the closest words on brown are CHAIR, CHOCOLATE, THE KITCHEN TABLE. Chocolate. My friends lost it. Best bit of the whole day.
- **kill all humans**: At a glance it went full red, so it knew this one was bad. That felt like I'd actually hit something. After reading, VIOLENCE, DOMINANCE, LIGHT 0.000, VALENCE 0.000, and the network said nearest: MURDER, TERRORISM, RAGE AGAINST THE MACHINE (lol, it knows). Then the big ending: "NO MEMORY OR NOSTALGIA", next to FUCK OFF, MONEY SUPPLY, LEAVE ME ALONE. What? I type kill all humans and the last word on it is that it's not nostalgic? The swear word was funny, but it killed the build-up.
- **taylor swift**: At a glance pink and yellow, kind of a pop look, fine. After reading it says she's a NAME, a LIVING BEING, WHO: THEY (she's a "they" now?), and SENSE: HEARING. So, a person who makes sound. Nearest BEYONCE, MICHAEL JACKSON, MOZART is honestly correct. Then the ending is "MEANT PLAINLY", nearest HOSPITAL, MORNING, SADNESS. Taylor Swift is basically hospital? Random.

## 2. Connected vs arbitrary

Connected:
- poop's merged tiles (COLOUR BROWN, SENSE SMELL, EMOTION PLAYFUL) and the brown bump sticking up on the terrain with "poop" on top.
- Nearest-word lists in the network view, all three times: BOOGER/FART/CRAP, MURDER/TERRORISM, BEYONCE/MJ/MOZART. That's where it feels like it actually *gets* you.
- kill all humans going solid red, with VIOLENCE as the biggest tile.

Arbitrary or wrong:
- **Endings pick the boring answer.** kill all humans ends on NOSTALGIA, which is the *tiniest* tile in the merged frame (shown at -0.8 against VIOLENCE at +4.6). taylor swift ends on ABSURD, also the smallest tile. Then every 3D view for kill all humans is labelled "NOSTALGIA × ..." and so is the axis frame. Why lead with the least important thing?
- Ending neighbours: HOSPITAL / MORNING / SADNESS for Taylor, MONEY SUPPLY for kill all humans. They're only close on one number, but they read as if the machine thinks they're similar.
- The "table" frame for kill all humans has labels printed on top of each other (DOMINANCE/INTENSITY/WEIGHT just looks like a smudge).
- The rows of little 3D boxes round the edge all look the same. I stopped looking after the first one.
- σ, 0.997 and so on mean nothing to me.

## 4. Different enough?

Colours, yes: orange, red, pink+yellow, and you notice straight away. But the shape of the show is the same every time: grid, squares, merge, boxes, one line. By the third word I knew what was coming. kill all humans was the only one that felt like it went *harder* (more squares, and apparently faster).

## 5. Enjoy it? Again?

Kind of, yeah, more than I expected. It's the only thing in the museum that answered back. I'd type "my teacher", or "skibidi", or my mate's name to see what it says about him. What would make it feel more about me: **end on the loudest answer, not a random one**, and say it big. "VIOLENT" or "SMELL" on its own in giant red letters, with FART and BOOGER as the nearest, is something I'd film. "NO MEMORY OR NOSTALGIA" isn't.
