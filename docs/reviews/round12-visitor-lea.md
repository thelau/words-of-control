# Round 12 — simulated visitor: Lea

Lea, 27, freelance illustrator. She goes to new media shows a lot and wants two things: is it beautiful, does it move her.
She typed "the sea at night", "climate change", "i cheated on her". (Reviewed from the frames only. The sound is described
in the brief but I could not hear it.)

## 1. What it seemed to say

- **the sea at night.** First impression: a blue word, liquid, flowing, the dark one. After reading: water 0.999, liquid,
  flowing, pulsing, light 0.002, and an ending that reads DARK with **DESPAIR · CRUELTY · DEATH** as my nearest words.
  Everything up to the ending was a portrait of the sea. The ending turned it into a portrait of despair.
- **climate change.** First impression: green and blue, big, abstract, nature. After reading: scale 0.987, abstract idea,
  domain nature, closeness 0.010, absurd 0.020. The network gave GLOBAL WARMING · POLLUTION · CAPITALISM, and the ending
  said VAST with MOON · EARTH · AWE.
- **i cheated on her.** First impression: red, broken, it knows. After reading: motion breaking, texture cracked, time past,
  who: I, daytime night. The nearest words were MY HUSBAND CHEATS · MY FRIEND BETRAYED ME · MY WIFE LEFT ME.

## 2. Connected vs arbitrary

**Connected:**
- The sea terrain frame. A dotted white surface with one blue peak, labelled SHAPE: FLOWING. It is the most beautiful
  thing in the piece, and it actually looks like a swell at night. I'd buy a print.
- The sea cloud. The vertical stems fall like rain, and my word sits high up next to OCEAN and DEEP WATER.
- The red merge for "cheated". BREAKING, CRACKED, PAST, I, NIGHT, all in flat red tiles. It reads like a confession set
  as a Swiss poster. **WHO: I** in red was the moment it hit me. It knew the guilty one was me.
- CLOSENESS 0.010 lit up green for climate change. Intended or not, that is the political point: nobody feels it close.
- CAPITALISM as a neighbour. Sharp.

**Arbitrary or wrong:**
- **DESPAIR, CRUELTY, DEATH for the sea at night.** They are my neighbours only because they are also dark. It felt like
  being told my poem was morbid. It is the worst moment of my three, and it is the last thing I saw.
- The climate ending, VAST with MOON · EARTH · AWE. It's pretty, but it drained the politics out. The network said
  pollution and capitalism, and the ending said awe. The piece picked the most harmless answer to end on.
- The cheating neighbours are all victims' sentences: my husband cheats, my wife left me, my friend betrayed me, I'm
  heartbroken. I'm the one who did it. The grid saw "I" and the ending forgot it.
- "the sea at night · POSITIVE 0.49" in the corner. A cold half-score on a poem.
- The 3×3 ring of tiny scatter plots (LIGHT × SHAPE × DAYTIME…) around the 3D views. They're beautiful as texture but
  impossible to read, and they look identical across all three words.

## 3. Correlation scores

- the sea at night: **3**. It would be a 4 without the ending.
- climate change: **3**
- i cheated on her: **4**

## 4. Different enough?

The colour does most of the work: blue, then green and blue, then red. The red one felt like mine. The structure is the
same every time: grid, mark, merge, net, axis, quote. By the third word I knew what was coming. The sea got a longer show
(cloud, terrain, ridges), and that made it feel considered. The other two felt shorter and more routine.

## 5. Enjoy it?

Yes, as an object. The flat colour on black is gorgeous and the typography is disciplined. It moved me once, at WHO: I.
I'd type another: "my mother's hands".

**One change:** let the ending's nearest words come from the answers that set my word apart, not from the one extreme
answer. The sea should end among ocean and deep water, not death. The confession should end near words that share its
"I", not near the victims.
