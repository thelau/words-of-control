# Round 12 — simulated visitor: Marta

*Persona: Marta, 34, nurse, Saturday visit with a friend. Her father died fourteen months ago. Not into tech or art
jargon, a bit wary of AI. Typed, in order: "my dad died last year", "i'm happy today", "je t'aime".*

## 1. What it seemed to say

- **"my dad died last year"**: first impression, "this is heavy, it's over, it's black and white". After reading:
  LOSS 0.990, TIME PAST, COLOUR BLACK, WEIGHT, and the nearest were "I lost my father, my mother is dying". So it understood.
  Then the last screen says **ABSURD 0.01** under my dad's death, and that is the word I walked out with.
- **"i'm happy today"**: first impression, "yellow, sunny, nice". After reading: yellow, light, rising, present, and
  the nearest were sun, star, joy. True, but it's what a greeting card would say.
- **"je t'aime"**: first impression, "red and pink, love". After reading: INTIMATE, distance zero, tender, soft,
  and it put me next to "mother, I love you, lover". It understood the French. That got me.

## 2. Connected vs. arbitrary

**Connected:**
- The marked grid for my dad. LOSS, TIME PAST and COLOUR BLACK lit up, and those are right. Some of the small dark
  labels were the truest thing in the whole piece: TEXTURE CRACKED, MOTION FALLING, RHYTHM DWINDLING, DAYTIME NIGHT,
  DOMAIN HOME. They went dark and nobody will ever read them. I wish they had stayed.
- "NEAREST: I LOST MY FATHER, MY MOTHER IS DYING". Strange to see my sentence placed among other people's, but it felt
  less lonely.
- In "je t'aime", "MOTHER" as the nearest word, and WHO: YOU. The pink and red tiles felt warm, like a valentine.
- In "i'm happy today", LONELINESS lit up yellow at 0.06. After typing about my dad, that was oddly pointed.

**Arbitrary or wrong:**
- **The ending for my dad.** "MEANT PLAINLY / ABSURD 0.01". I know it means "not absurd", but putting the word ABSURD
  under a death is cruel. Also "HUMAN RIGHTS" as one of my three nearest? No.
- The white merged tiles for my dad looked exactly like a lab report: values, flags, "+2.7σ". I read those all day
  at work. Grief as a printout with **NEGATIVE 0.83** at the top felt cold, like my father was getting a score.
- The colour said BLACK but the cells turned white. Confusing.
- The cubes and the many small dot plots around the edge (ABSURD × LOSS × IRONY…). Pretty, but for me they were
  just snow. By the third word I stopped looking at them.
- I mostly remember the pictures. The sound for my dad was faster and clickier than I expected, not sad.

## 3. Correlation scores

- "my dad died last year": **4** (it clearly understood; the ending pulls it down)
- "i'm happy today": **3** (anyone's happy would get yellow and sun)
- "je t'aime": **4**

## 4. Different enough?

The colours, yes: white on black, then yellow, then red and pink. I could tell my three apart from across the room.
But the shape of it is the same every time: grid, tiles, cube, the edge plots, the line of dots. By the third word I
knew what came next and was only waiting for the colour and the last words.

## 5. Did I enjoy it?

Partly. The dad one hit me, half in a good way (it knew) and half in a bad way (the printout, the "absurd").
"Je t'aime" I liked the most. I'd type one more, maybe "papa" on its own, to see if it's softer than the sentence.
My friend would type something silly.

**One change:** let the ending say the true thing in plain words. LOSS, or even CRACKED or DWINDLING, not a "no" to
a word like ABSURD. And show the little words (falling, night, home) bigger; they were more about me than any number.
