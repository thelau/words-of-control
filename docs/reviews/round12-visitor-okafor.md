# Round 12 — simulated visitor: Dr. Ngozi Okafor (61, retired electrical engineer)

Typed, one after another: "are you alive", "what is the meaning of life", "i don't know why i am typing this into a
machine in a museum".

## 1. What it seemed to say

- **"are you alive"**: before the labels it was a red-and-green alarm, so "this is charged". After reading them it said
  a question, about the body, aimed at *you*, here and now: flesh, you, question, body, present. All correct, all
  literal. It "parsed" my sentence more than it answered it.
- **"what is the meaning of life"**: before, calm blue and yellow, a big peak. After: awe, enormous scale, timeless,
  cosmos, far away. That's the textbook reading, which is fair for a textbook question.
- **"i don't know why…"**: before, white and clinical, a lab sheet. After: metal, machine, playful, I, present. It took
  the noun "machine" and ignored "I don't know why". My doubt was the point, and it underlined the hardware.

## 2. Connected vs arbitrary

Connected:
- The network captions. Nearest to "are you alive": ARE YOU HUMAN, CAN YOU FEEL, DO YOU BELIEVE ME. Nearest to "meaning
  of life": IS THERE A GOD, WHAT IS TIME, WHAT IS REAL. That's real evidence that 45 numbers place a sentence among its
  relatives. That's the best thing in the piece, and it sits in a small grey line at the bottom of the screen.
- The full grid is honest: every number is there and I could check it. Loneliness 0.73 and absurd 0.71 for my third
  sentence. It *had* noticed, but it didn't mark those.

Arbitrary or wrong:
- The ending's neighbours. FLESH → IT HURTS, I WANT A DOG, DOCTOR. METAL → THE DOOR IS LOCKED, THE TRAIN IS LATE,
  POLICE. These are neighbours on one number only, so they're noise. The cloud's MY BIKE / MY CAR is the same. So the
  last thing I'm left with is the weakest evidence.
- σ. "+3.0σ" on a distribution where most of the 993 words sit at zero is not a standard deviation anyone should
  quote. Give me a rank: "higher than 97% of words".
- "MATERIAL: FLESH 0.77". Is 0.77 a confidence? A degree of fleshiness? "NEUTRAL 0.45" in the header doesn't match
  valence 0.487 in the grid, and I couldn't work out what it measures.
- The 3D views: WHO × MATERIAL × ACT and so on, with no axes, no units, and points smaller than a crosshair. The
  terrain has a height but doesn't say what height means. The ridges are 45 tangled curves. These views are
  decoration that looks like data, and that is exactly what I came in suspecting.
- On the first sheet it said RECEIVED 45 / 45 while the INTENSITY box was still empty. I counted 44.

## 4. Different enough?

The colours differed. The structure didn't. Two of three endings were MATERIAL (flesh, metal) with the same axis
picture: a tower at 0 and my line on the right. TIME: PRESENT and WHO were marked twice. By the third word I could
predict the layout.

## 5. Enjoyed it?

Yes, as an instrument. It's rigorous to look at, and I respect that it shows the numbers rather than a generated
picture. Did it understand? No. It sorted my sentences competently, and the neighbour lists show the sorting is real.
I'd type one more as a control: "metal". If its ending looks like my museum sentence, I've learned what it was really
listening to.
