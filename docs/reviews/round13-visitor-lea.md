# Round 13 — visitor: Lea (27, freelance illustrator)

Typed, one after another: "the sea at night", "climate change", "i cheated on her".

## 1. What it seemed to say

- **the sea at night.** Before reading: it's blue, it's calm, it's water. After reading: liquid, flowing, water,
  pulsing, almost no light. It understood the sea and mostly dropped the night. The ending says LIQUID, which is the
  most literal answer it had.
- **climate change.** Before reading: green and blue, two colours fighting, so something complicated. After
  reading: huge scale, long duration, nature, far away, anxiety, an abstract idea. Correct, like a good encyclopedia
  entry. It ends on ANXIETY, which I already knew.
- **i cheated on her.** Before reading: red, fast, broken. After reading: breaking, cracked, past, action, night,
  WHO: I. It took my words seriously. It ends on BREAKING, which is the right word.

## 2. Connected vs arbitrary

Connected:
- The red grid for the confession. It went red at once and six cells lit up. **WHO: I** lit in red, and that made
  me feel caught. TEXTURE: CRACKED and TIME: PAST read like a sentence about me.
- The terrain for the sea: a white mesh with one blue swell on the corner, labelled with my words. That is the most
  beautiful frame in the whole piece, and the only one that felt drawn rather than plotted.
- Nearest for climate change: GLOBAL WARMING, POLLUTION, **CAPITALISM**. Capitalism made me smile, because it has a
  position.

Arbitrary or wrong:
- **i cheated on her → MY HUSBAND CHEATS, MY FRIEND BETRAYED ME, MY WIFE LEFT ME.** Every neighbour is someone
  who was hurt. I confessed to doing it, and it put me among the victims. No guilt and no shame anywhere in the 45
  answers. That is the biggest miss, because it's exactly the thing I would never say out loud.
- The sea's "night" ended up as the thinnest sliver in the merge (LIGHT, LOW 3%, tiny type), while LIQUID got the
  big tile. The darkness was the poem, and it got the smallest space.
- Climate change filled in green and blue, but its ending is all blue. Where did my green go? Also MATERIAL: ICE.
- In the sea's network, my words' label sits on top of the lines and I can't read it. The confession's network
  frame drops the grid and turns into a big tilted cube, which looks like a different piece.
- The rows of small 3D views around the edge (RHYTHM × TEXTURE × KIND…) are texture to me, not meaning. They're
  pretty dust.

## 4. Different enough?

Mostly by colour: blue, green+blue, red. That works, and the red really did hit me. But the structure is the same
every time: grid, merge, a cloud of white dots, a dot plot with three names. By the third word I knew the choreography.
The ending looks almost identical each time, with the same line of dots, the same layout and only a new label.

## 5. Enjoy it?

Yes. The black and white with one flat colour is beautiful, very Ikeda, and the moment the grid clears to only the
marked cells has real tension. It moved me once, on the red WHO: I. The rest I admired more than felt.

I'd type "my mother's hands" to see whether it finds tenderness or just skin.

One change: let the ending land on the answer that is most *mine*, not the most extreme one. For the sea that's the
darkness, not LIQUID. For the confession, give it a way to tell the person who did it from the person it was done to.
The neighbours are where I either feel seen or feel filed away.
