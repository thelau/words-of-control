# Words of Control: curator's review, round 2

## Verdict

It now reads as a work at the level of the **grammar**, but not yet at the level of the **vocabulary**. Appraisal, then verdict, then black, then cursor is a real ritual. The hard cut to black with a reverb tail is exactly right: the machine delivers its judgement and leaves the room. The stance ("the AI only appraises, and everything you see is authored") is legible in the appraisal. You watch something *read* your word: hex, floats, bit-grids, then a single line. The white inverted numbers frame (knife-01, scream-01) is the best 100 ms in the piece.

It is not at the bar yet. Of the four clip families, two (fracture and grains) are the old central sun redrawn as lines. Fuck, knife, scream and fire resolve to the **same radial starburst**. Goodbye, almost, ocean and fire open on a **warp-speed radial burst**. That is the "big bang" Laurent rejected, and the typing starfield I flagged in round 1, now arriving from the other side of Enter. Half of the fourteen words end in a firework. A visitor who types two angry words will see the machine's whole range in about 20 seconds, and it will look like a screensaver with a good intro.

## Strongest

- **The appraisal sequence.** Horizontal spectra (fuck, ocean), the bit-grid (kiss, glass, fire), the byte columns and the bracketed scatter box (dust, 海) are rigorous, monochrome, with one accent, and they are *specific*. The accent colour taken from Jev (pink for mother/kiss, red for fuck/knife, yellow for afternoon, orange for fire) is the right amount of feeling.
- **The line.** Every appraisal ends on one thin horizontal line. It works as a verdict, a flatline and a horizon at once. This is the hinge of the grammar and deserves more weight (see change 4).
- **Relief (mother, kiss).** Raking light on carved matter is the nearest thing here to the monolith video and `sand-rings.jpg`. It is matter, not emission.
- **Indifference (nothing, afternoon).** A diagonal shaft of warm, dusty light drifting across black. This is the first image that feels *authored rather than rendered*, and it is the correct answer to "a lazy afternoon". It is quiet, off-centre and unhurried, and it carries a hint of shutters and heat.
- **Dust (grains-b)** and **goodbye (grains-b)** show fibrous, curling flow with no centre. That is what "lit granular matter" should look like, and it is what the grains family should *be*.

## Weakest (precise)

- **Fracture = starburst** (fuck 03/07, knife 03–08, scream 03–10, fire 03/07). All the lines meet at one point, with salmon glow. It reads as fireworks or a hyperspace star, not breakage. Glass (03–04) is the only version that works, because it is sparse, white, fine and has a hairline web. Even there, the impact point sits dead centre.
- **Grains-a = warp burst** (fuck 05/09, fire 05/09, goodbye 05, almost 03/06, ocean 03). The ocean opener is a literal glowing sun in a starfield. Almost-07 has an unexplained bright blob welling up from the bottom edge, which reads as a render artifact.
- **Relief is a Rorschach face in a glowing box.** The mirror symmetry produces masks and demons (mother-05 has eyes and a mouth). The soft halo around the square makes it a lit screen, not a slab. Mother and kiss get the *same* clip with the same palette, and nothing visible separates them.
- **Haze = plasma screensaver** (ocean 04–07, 海 03–06). Wavy caustic lines tile uniformly and barely move between the early and late frames. Ocean spends two shots in the same haze, so "awe" becomes wallpaper.
- **Nothing and afternoon are identical.** The idea is right, but "nothing" (arousal 0.02, void 100) should be *less* than afternoon, not the same.

## Ritual

The grammar holds. The problem is density *inside* the verdict. Shots are long single states, and the early and late frames barely differ. Ikeda's authority comes from rhythm at the edges of perception. Here the appraisal has that rhythm and the verdict loses it. The verdict should be edited as hard as the appraisal is.

## Ranked changes

1. **Remove every centre-emitting radial form.** Fracture should become a crack *travelling across a surface*, from an off-centre origin, with refraction, dark and slow, then a snap. Grains should become the dust/goodbye flow field, never the burst. The centre rule means *composed around* the centre, not *radiating from* it.
2. **Make the director's choices visible per word.** Material and shape have to change the image, not just the palette. Knife (metal, point, strike) should not look like scream (flesh, voice) or fire (rising, spreading). Before adding anything else, test pairs that must differ: fuck/scream, mother/kiss, ocean/海.
3. **Relief: break the mirror, kill the halo, move the light.** Use asymmetric carving and a slab edge that is hard and dark. Most importantly, the raking light should *sweep* during the shot (the monolith video), so the matter is revealed rather than displayed. Kiss could be two surfaces nearly touching. Mother could be a single slow light rising.
4. **Promote the line.** Let the final appraisal line *become* the verdict: it tilts, breaks, thickens or goes slack (for nothing, it simply fades). Its length and angle should come from valence and dominance. That single transformation shows the audience that the judgement is driving the image.
5. **Cut the verdict like the appraisal.** Use cuts under 300 ms inside shots at high arousal, insert inverted white frames, and let a shot fail to resolve at low confidence. Almost (confidence 0.39) should stutter, not dissolve.
6. **Split indifference.** Afternoon should be *slatted* light: bars of warmth drifting slowly across the frame, like shutters, with a faint heat-shimmer. Make it longer (15–25 s), long enough that a watcher gets bored and stays anyway. Nothing should be almost nothing: the line, a single mote, black. The machine declines to perform.

## New clip families

1. **Chladni / plate.** Sand on a vibrating plate, lit from the side, organising into nodal figures driven by *the actual frequencies of the drone and the verdict sound*. This is the literal "plate instrument" made visible, grounded in `sand-rings.jpg`, and it is the most honest sound=image object you could build. Order, symmetry and pitch map directly. Low confidence gives figures that never settle.
2. **Eclipse / aperture.** Taken from video-a's cracked disc: a *dark* core, rim-lit grains around a void, an iris that contracts or tears. Fear gets a tiny aperture under 5% of frame height. Awe gets a vast, slow withdrawal. It corrects the sun cliché while keeping the reference Laurent loves.
3. **Furrow / rake.** A comb draws parallel grooves through a grain bed. The tines are the word's own bytes, so every word carves a unique, exact texture. It connects the Ikeda barcode of the appraisal to matter in the verdict: data becomes relief, which is the move in the monolith video.

## The second word

People type again when they suspect the machine *discriminated*. That requires (a) visible difference between neighbouring words (change 2), (b) visible evidence that the judgement drove the result (change 4), and (c) memory: let the room at rest keep a faint residue of the last verdict (a groove, a grain drift, a colour in the black) that the next word disturbs. Watchers guessing the word from the screen will do the rest.
