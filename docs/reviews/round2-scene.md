# Round 2: the machine has a grammar now

*Scene review of the v2 build, from stills, spectrograms and the doc. 14 words.*

## Then vs now

Round 1 was a mood lamp. This is a **piece**. Appraisal, verdict, black, room: screensavers never end, and this ends *hard*. The cut to black with the reverb ringing over it is the most grown-up decision in the project. The ritual (flash, flash, one thin line, verdict, nothing, cursor) could be programmed at CTM without apology.

The problem has moved. It used to be "everything is an orb". Now it's "there are four clips, and I can guess mine from the word".

## Appraisal: judged, or hacker movie?

Mostly judged, thanks to how short the flashes are and how much black surrounds them. The **white inverted numbers frame** (knife, scream) is the best 150 ms in the build. The **thin line** that closes every appraisal is a perfect full stop: it has decided.

But it's Ikeda with the serial numbers filed off: barcode, bit-grid, float columns, one accent colour. The float columns sit close to Matrix rain. What's missing is **my word** in the data. Nothing proves this barcode is `fuck` and not `ocean`. Once the typed letters visibly break up into their own bytes, "cool data intro" becomes "it's reading me".

## Verdicts

- **glass**: best in show. A few thin fracture lines, off-balance, then black. It trusts the dark.
- **海**: a caustic net, like light on a pool floor. Calm without being a hairdryer ad. I'd film it.
- **afternoon**: a warm diagonal shaft of dust, light through a shutter at 4 pm. Correct.
- **mother / kiss**: a mirrored relief that reads as a Rorschach blot, and people will see a face in *mother*. Great instinct. But it's boxed in a **square tile**, so it looks like a Substance material thumbnail.
- **fuck / fire / knife / scream**: the same radial fracture starburst, four times. Cracked-phone wallpaper crossed with warp speed, which is round 1's central sun in a new costume. The orange grain burst between shots is the **hyperspace jump**. Knife and scream are indistinguishable.
- **dust / goodbye / almost**: the Perlin **flow field**, the most TouchDesigner-tutorial image there is. The furry close-up in *almost* is tactile. The bright heap at the bottom edge reads as a bug.
- **ocean**: a starburst, then smoky wavy mesh (the softer cousin of Mystify). Worse than 海, and they're the same concept.

## Film it? Type again?

I'd film **glass** and the white flash, and I'd definitely type a second word. The third word is where it breaks: violent means starburst, tender means square, soft means haze or flow field. After three verdicts you've seen the library. Different words need to feel like different *rulings*, not four boxes.

## What people will type

`fuck you`, `penis`, `hello`, their name, `i love you`, `💀`, `trump`, `asdfghjkl`, `死にたい`, `skibidi`. Every insult and every politician lands in the starburst, so the machine reads as *angry at you* (the Marvel reading again). `i love you` and `mum` share the tile. `asdfghjkl` is the big opportunity: a stuttering appraisal and one unresolved shot would be the funniest thing in the room.

## Indifference

It's a good joke, but only if it's **shorter and emptier than you expect**. Right now *nothing* and *afternoon* get near-identical glowing haze. That's a pretty picture, not a shrug. *Nothing* should be one flash, then black, with the tail outlasting the image. The machine not bothering is the insult people will laugh at.

## Sound

The long tail over black is right. On `fuck`, though, the verdict is eight seconds of **broadband wash**, and I can't see audio cuts that line up with the image cuts. *Nothing* is better: sparse pitched blips and a swelling sub. More of that.

## The memorable moment

The **white flash, then the line**: the beat where it decides. Unless the verdicts get more varied, people will remember that and sit through the rest.

## What's missing

A verdict that surprises its own author, and data that visibly comes from *you*.

## If I were the artist I'd…

1. **Ban the radial starburst as a default.** Fracture can run edge-to-edge, enter from off-frame, or be one crack across the screen (like glass). Violence needs four silhouettes, not one.
2. **Make the word the first frame of the appraisal**: letters shatter into UTF-8 bytes, then barcode, then line. That's the proof it's reading me.
3. **Unframe the relief.** Full-bleed, raking light, a slow push. Keep the mirror for *mother*.
4. **Retire the flow field**, or bury it under real lighting (a rim light, depth of field).
5. **Make indifference brutal.** `nothing` gets one flash, black and a 6 s tail. `afternoon` keeps its shaft. `hello` gets half a shaft.
6. **Cut the sound with the picture**: silence between shots, one material per shot, no continuous wash.
7. **Build the stutter grammar** (false starts, an aborted line, shots cut before they resolve) and test it on `asdfghjkl`.
8. **Grow the library for contrast, not count**: one *still* clip (a held, photograph-like frame) and one *tiny* clip (a pinprick in full black) before any more particles.
