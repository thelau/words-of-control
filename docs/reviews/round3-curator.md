# Words of Control: curator's review, round 3

## Verdict: 6.5/10 (round 2: about 5)

The vocabulary has caught up with the grammar in places. It has not caught up in the middle of the range. The warp-speed burst has gone from the openers. **Nothing** is now a single mote in the black: the machine declines to perform, which is exactly right. **Afternoon** is slatted shutter light with dust. It is quiet, warm and off-centre, and it is the best shot in the piece; I would show it tomorrow. **Knife** now opens on one diagonal cleave with a shaft of light, and that reads as *knife*. **Scream** cuts to full-frame cracked earth under raking light: matter, not emission. **Thunder**'s macro of the sand plate (frames 09–10) is the first plate image with real scale. The appraisal family has grown: the wireframe box for nothing, asdfgh and thunder is rigorous and new.

It is not at the bar. Two round-2 failures have survived under new names. Fracture still radiates from a hub (fuck, fire, thunder and scream shot 0). Grains-a is still a firework (fuck, fire). The plate has become the new default, turning up in 6 of 15 words (fuck, mother, ocean, goodbye, forever, thunder), almost always as the same glowing square tile with a diagonal crease. Round-2 change 4 (promote the line) has not been touched: every word ends on the same grey line.

## Connection per word

- **Strong:** nothing, afternoon, glass (sparse, white, a single shot), dust, knife (the cleave), 海 (its caustic close-ups beat ocean's, and sharing water with ocean is the right cross-language rhyme).
- **Partial:** scream (the earth works, but the orange spoked fracture is generic), ocean (the caustics connect, but the opening plate tile does not), goodbye (the dim caustics fade well, then a bright plate contradicts them), asdfgh (the green scan is arbitrary, though the dim shutters are a nice shrug), thunder (it has matter but no strike: there is never one frame of white).
- **Weak:** fuck (fracture, sparks, plate, fracture: a sampler, not a judgement; the plate shows two eyes), fire (fire is not broken glass; amber spokes plus a spark fountain reads as welding), mother (the crumpled-paper relief is promising, then the plate draws eyes again), forever (a symmetric ceramic tile with eyes, a souvenir rather than eternity).

## Five fixes, ranked

1. **Kill the hub.** In every fracture shot except knife and glass, the lines still meet at one point, and a small white shard-cluster sprite sits there (knife 05/07, fire 07, thunder 07, scream shot 3). It reads as a UI icon. Remove the sprite. Start the crack off-frame and let it *travel*, as knife's cleave does. The visible disc edge (fuck 03, fire 03, glass 03, scream 03) also gives away a round mesh, so extend the surface past the frame. Fire should never pick fracture: remap it to grains, haze or heat-shimmer.
2. **Fix grains-a (fuck, fire).** The spark fountain is still a firework, and it is clipped by a hard rectangular edge (fuck 05–06, fire 05–06 show a box corner). This is a render bug visible from across a room. Sparks should fall under gravity from a single contact off-centre, sparse and short-lived, never as a fan.
3. **Ration and de-tile the plate.** Six of fifteen is a house style, not a verdict. Cap it at affinity to sand, sound or symmetry. Remove the glow halo and the recurring diagonal crease. Break the mirror, because the D4 modes keep drawing faces (fuck 07, mother 05, forever 03). Frame it the way thunder 09–10 does: full-bleed, grazing light, grains visible. Take it out of fuck entirely.
4. **Promote the line (carried over).** The final appraisal line is identical for all 15 words. Let it become the first verdict frame. For knife it becomes the cleave. For nothing it goes slack and fades. For thunder it snaps white for 2 frames. For goodbye it drifts down and off. Set its angle and length from valence and dominance. This is the cheapest proof that the judgement drives the image.
5. **Give thunder and scream their strike, and drop the scan cliché.** High arousal needs one inverted white frame or a sub-300 ms cut (round-2 change 5 is still not visible). The knife scan (red stacked ridgelines) is *Unknown Pleasures*, and the first quote anyone in the room will name. Make the scan a single laser line that reveals, then forgets: no stacked profiles, and no green for asdfgh.
