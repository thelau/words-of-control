# Round 3: the rulings got sharper, the library got boxier

*Scene review from the review3 contact sheets (15 words) and DIRECTION-v2. Stills only, no audio this round.*

**Score: 7/10** (round 2: ~6). The grammar was already right. This round the extremes got braver. The middle is where it's weak now.

## What changed

- **The word opens the appraisal**, with its hex bytes underneath. That's the proof it's reading me. But the bytes are 6 px and never *become* the barcode. Right now it's a caption.
- **Indifference is now brutal.** `nothing` is a wireframe box, a line, then a near-black frame with one speck. Best joke in the build. `afternoon` got a louvred shutter shaft, warm and tilted. That's the south of France.
- **Glass and knife got the single cleave.** One seam, one raking bar of light, a lot of dark. Very good.
- **The flow field is almost gone** (only `dust` still has it, underexposed).
- **Two new clips, and both are clichés.** *Scan* is a stack of Unknown Pleasures lines (red on knife, oscilloscope green on asdfgh). *Plate* is the Chladni tile, which is round 2's square-thumbnail problem again, only more common.

## Would I type again?

Second word, yes. Third word, maybe, and then I'd leave. The **square tile** shows up in 7 of 15 words (fuck, mother, ocean, goodbye, forever, thunder, plus scream's slab). The **radial starburst with the shard cluster at centre** is still in fuck, fire, scream and thunder. By word three the room knows the deck.

## Lands

- **nothing**: the machine shrugs. People will laugh, then type something rude to get a reaction. That's the loop you want.
- **afternoon**: correct and unhurried.
- **glass / knife (cleave shots)**: confident and minimal. Film these.
- **海**: the caustics are still gorgeous. The macro second shot is a real cinematographic move.
- **thunder's last shot**: the plate **full-bleed and close**, raked light, grains glinting. It's the best plate frame in the set, and it proves the tile shouldn't be a tile.
- **scream's cracked-relief close-up**: tactile, violent without being a starburst.

## Falls flat

- **fuck**: starburst, then sparks as warp speed, then a tile, then another starburst. It's a screensaver sampler.
- **mother / forever / goodbye**: tile, tile, tile. `forever` gets a Rorschach ornament that looks like a bathroom azulejo.
- **asdfgh**: a green scope line and a dim shaft, all calm. The nonsense word should glitch, and nothing is stuttering yet.
- **ocean**: tile, caustics, tile. It loses to 海 again.
- **dust**: the image is dim and muddy, which isn't the same as quiet.
- **Wireframe box frames** (nothing, asdfgh, thunder): they look like a CAD viewport, weaker than the barcode frames.

## Five fixes, ranked

1. **Kill the frame around plate and relief.** Always full-bleed, at macro distance, with raking light and a slow push. Take thunder's last shot as the template. Allow at most one framed tile per verdict, and only when symmetry is high (mother).
2. **Retire the centred starburst for good.** Fracture gets exactly two forms, the single cleave and the edge-entry shatter (scream's 6th frame). Move the impact point off-centre and let it leave frame. Drop the shard cluster.
3. **Build the stutter.** Low confidence or nonsense should get a false start, a line that aborts halfway and shots cut at 300 ms before they resolve. Maybe a hard freeze-frame too. asdfgh should be the funniest 3 seconds in the room.
4. **Let the word visibly become the data.** The letters explode into their bytes, the bytes stretch into the barcode, the barcode collapses into the line. That's one continuous gesture in 1.5 s, not four separate slides. Replace the wireframe-box frame with it.
5. **Rework or bench scan.** Stacked waveform lines are a record-sleeve reference everyone has seen. Show the laser *finding* a hidden form instead: one bright line sweeping, with a face or edge lit only where it passes, gone behind it. Monochrome, no green.
