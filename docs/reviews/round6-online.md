# Round 6 — online visitors (simulated)

Source: `review9/sheet-*.png` (18 words) and `audio/spectrum-*.png`. Stills and
spectrograms only: motion and loudness are inferred.

## Two facts under every persona

- **The sound lives below 120 Hz.** In every spectrogram, *spoon* included, the loudest band is 20–110 Hz.
  Above it there's only a faint 6–18 kHz hiss and a few sines. Phone speakers roll off around 150–200 Hz,
  so a phone plays the verdict as hiss and beeps.
- **Most frames are dim bokeh on near-black.** At phone brightness, grief 5–8, love 5–6, *i miss you* 6–9
  and rage 9–11 look like an empty screen. The frames that land are the bright ones: the cream membranes
  (war, fuck, storm, *looks like crap*), the relief slabs, the barcodes and the bit grid.

## Personas

**1. Maya, 19, comes from a TikTok clip on her phone at 1 a.m. with the sound off.** She taps the black page
and no keyboard appears. When one does, she types *love* and gets numbers, a line, then dim pink bokeh that
looks broken. *i miss you*: a lovely bit grid, then 4 s of black. Portrait crops
the frame. She tries 2 words and records nothing. **Comment:** "is it supposed to be black??"

**2. Jonas, a motion designer on desktop with headphones.** He types *grief*, *war*, *storm*, *spoon*,
*qwerty* and *kiss*. The subs hit him in the chest; the war Chladni run is the best thing here. But spoon,
qwerty, 42 and table all give the same light blade. Storm is war in blue, and *kiss* gets *war*'s cracked
slab. He tries 8 words and posts 12 s of war. **Caption:** "typed 'war' into a machine that only judges. it
answered in sand."

**3. Dex, a streamer, with chat typing words live.** Chat sends *baby*, *fuck*, *rage*, *skibidi* and their
handles. On stream, 3–5 s of black is dead air, and chat spams "???". *fuck* goes over well (the cream card,
then the slab). *baby* gets a pink crystal block, and chat says it looks like a virus. He does about 12 words
in 8 minutes. Chat can't see what the machine *thought*, so there's nothing to argue about. **Chat:** "the AI
hates dex."

**4. Sam, testing a friend's name and then insults.** *Tom* gets the neutral blade, and Sam feels nothing.
*looks like crap* gets cream membrane close-ups around a black hole, as if the machine flinched. If BARRED
cuts *Tom is a loser* instantly, that refusal is what Sam screenshots. 6 words. **Comment:** "it
ignored me lol, respect."

**5. Aiko, typing in Japanese and then French.** She types *悲しみ* and *amour*, and likes the hex bytes
under the glyphs. If *悲しみ* gets grief's cold blue barcode and lattice, she feels understood across
languages. She shares a side-by-side of *amour* and *love*. **Caption:** "same feeling, two languages, one
judgement." If the two differ a lot, it reads as a bug.

## Across personas

- **Hook:** the barcode or bit grid in the first second works. The first verdict frame is almost always a
  distant grey cloud (frame 4 on every sheet), the weakest image on a phone.
- **Repetition:** after 5 words, it's predictable. Negative words run lines, sand, slab. Neutral words get
  the blade. Baby, mother and *i miss you* share one pink lattice. *nothing* and *silence* are the same
  void, which on a phone looks like a crash.
- **Sharing:** no frame names the word.
- **Being understood:** people feel understood on extreme words (war, grief, *looks like crap*), not on
  everyday words or names.

## Six changes, ranked

1. **Make it work on phones.** A mid-band harmonic (200 Hz–2 kHz) that tracks every sub impact. A mobile
   exposure floor. Tap-to-type that opens the keyboard. Portrait camera framings, not crops. Same
   verdict, now audible and visible.
2. **Open the verdict on its brightest frame.** Put a membrane, relief or starburst within 1.5 s of the
   line. Move the dim clouds later and shorten the near-black stretches. The first 3 s decide whether
   anyone records.
3. **Sign the last frame before black.** Hold 1 s on the word in small type, with its hex bytes and a
   serial. Every clip then carries its own caption.
4. **Make the refusal visible.** When BARRED fires, give it a wordless event: one hard white frame, one
   tone, then the cursor. People share that.
5. **Tie each verdict to its word.** Carry the appraisal's signatures into the 3D: barcode bars become
   geometry, and the bytes set the Chladni mode. *kiss* must not share *war*'s slab. Neutral words get
   authored shrugs, not the shared blade.
6. **An optional local "keep".** A 9:16, 10–12 s export of the last performance, mixed for phones and
   rendered in the browser on request. Nothing goes to a server, so the no-logging rule holds.
