# Round 7 — three people: did it answer *my* word?

## 1. The journalist

**"It reads you, then shows you its mood ring"**

*Words of Control* is disciplined and beautiful: monochrome particle architecture, sand, a stone relief, a hard cut to black. The appraisal flash is the best 1.5 seconds in any AI artwork this year: a machine visibly taking your word apart. The verdict, though, is a shared repertoire with a tint applied. *Love* and *fire* get the same amber embers. *Grief* and *fear* get the same cold vertical rain. *Shame*, *fire* and *fear* all get the same cracked slab. The new material layer reads mostly as colour, sometimes as motion, almost never as rhythm. Where the reading is extreme (joy 0.99 light, ocean 1.00 water), it lands. Where the word is small (*tuesday*, *hello*, *why*), you get one elegant shape and a shrug. Gorgeous, but not yet a conversation.

## 2. The physical visitor (dark room, typewriter 6 m from the screen, ~15 min)

- **0:00 `hello`.** Arcs, crossing streaks, over quickly. Did it greet me? No. Pretty, but interchangeable.
- **1:00 `love`.** Amber starburst, embers, then a grey plaster slab. Why is love a wall? Half ✔.
- **2:00 `grief`.** Silver rain falling, ribbons, sand, then a near-30 s dim fade. The fall *and* the dwindling are both there. It felt like mourning. ✔
- **3:20 `shame`.** Jev read "contracting 0.95". I got a red-flecked radial fan, then the slab again. Nothing closed in on itself. ✘ Two slabs in three words: now I recognise the set.
- **4:10 `joy`.** A gold chandelier spiral, bright bokeh. Lovely. But the floor shook with grief's sub-thumps (spectrogram: 40–100 Hz impacts at 8–11 s). Joy doesn't thud. ✔ image, ✘ sound.
- **5:00 `maybe`.** Drifting grey columns, a web, horizontal threads. Drift reads. ✔ The best small word.
- **5:40 `why`.** A grey cloud and a wave. Jev heard "strike 0.82", but there was no single strike and no question. ✘
- **6:20 `tuesday`.** One lattice shot, then black. I laughed ("bored by Tuesday"), I think by accident.
- **7:00 `i miss you`.** Bars in a void, trailing lines, a blurred relief. It's treated as one word: "miss" and "you" are gone. ✘
- **8:30 `fire`, `ocean`.** Fire really rises. Ocean is blue bokeh and a slow snake. Now I am *testing* it, not saying anything.
- **11:00 `laurent`.** A calm, dim long take. Nothing marks it as a name.
- **12:00 bored.** I can predict the order: flash, bright particles, slab or sand, dim, black. The length varies. The story doesn't.
- **14:00.** One last `sorry`, then I leave.

**What stuck:** grief, fire, joy's chandelier. **What didn't:** everyday words, sentences, the slab.

## 3. The online visitor (phone, then laptop)

On a phone the appraisal's hex and hairlines are illegible. The dim clouds (`why`, `tuesday`, the grief tail) go near-black on OLED. `joy`, `love` and `fire` pop; I screen-recorded love's amber starburst and joy's gold spiral, 4 s each. On the laptop, grief vs shame differ by colour for 5 s (silver vs red flecks), then both become texture. I couldn't tell maybe from why blind. Hello and tuesday are both one geometric shot, then black: swappable. Laptop speakers lose the sub, so rhythm shrinks to a high tick pattern that's the same on every word. I typed 7 words. I'd share joy or fire, because the screen visibly *became* the word.

## Where the word is felt, and why

| Pair | Felt? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| grief / shame | grief yes, shame no | falling + dwindling are shown; contracting isn't. Both end on the slab. |
| joy / love | as colour only | gold vs amber bright particles. Love's flesh, spreading and steady aren't visible. |
| maybe / why | maybe yes | drift is a known behaviour. Strike and void aren't performed. |
| tuesday / hello | no | one shot, then black. Neither the greeting nor the mundane is authored. |

**Rule:** it works when one reading dominates (≥0.8) *and* has a motion we know from nature (rain falls, fire rises, smoke drifts). It fails when the reading is split, abstract (contracting, strike, still), or carried only in sound.

## Six changes, ranked

1. **Open on the dominant motion, done by the whole field.** For the first 3 s, every particle does one unmistakable gesture: shame collapses to a point, fear trembles, war shatters, joy lifts off the top edge. Today motion is a modifier on stock formations, so it gets lost.
2. **Rhythm is one event in sound and image, audible on laptops.** Strike means one hit, then silence. Pulse is a fixed heartbeat. Swelling is a crescendo with no sub. Dwindling makes each event sparser and quieter. Take the sub thumps away from light, rising and swelling words, and carry rhythm in the mid-band.
3. **Material changes the matter, not just its colour.** Fire is buoyant and flickers, water has a rippling surface, smoke is soft with no hard points, stone is a heavy block, metal gives sharp glints, void is almost empty. The slab only appears for stone, earth or metal readings, never for love, fire or shame.
4. **Make the word's letters countable in the matter.** The on-screen hex bytes set the number of columns, bursts or strikes and their spacing. A faint ghost of the typed word forms in the particles for one beat.
5. **Phrase-shaped verdicts for sentences and speech acts.** "i miss you" gets three beats, or a turn from void to water. Hello turns the field toward the viewer. Why holds a pitch that never resolves. A name is one lone point in a big field.
6. **Author the small words instead of shortening them.** Tuesday or ok gets a short, *deliberate* gesture: a metronomic tick and still metal, like office time. Brevity reads as a malfunction unless it has a clear shape.
