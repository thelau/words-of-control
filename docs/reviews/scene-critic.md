# Words of Control: a view from the queue

*Scene review, build of 2026-09-23. Based on stills, so I'm imagining the motion. There's a drone and key clicks, no reaction sound yet.*

## First impression

The setup is great. A black room, one blinking block, no wall text. Then you press Enter and, too often, you get a screensaver.

**Anger** is finished. The jagged shockwave, the full-frame streaks, embers hanging in the air: it reads as a *hit*, and it's the one people will film. The rest drift toward the same picture, a white-hot sun in the middle with concentric rings around it. Put the nine peak frames side by side and five of them are "pebble dropped in a pond".

## Fresh vs. seen-it

- **Anger**: fresh enough. The ring is a bit Marvel-portal, but the embers save it.
- **Sadness**: the only real gesture, because things *fall*. But the ring is a neon ring light and the tears drop in a straight-edged rectangle, like a shower curtain. Fix that and this becomes the one people remember.
- **Anxiety**: the wobbling contour lines are good (topo map, raked sand, close to `sand-rings.jpg`). The amber doughnut in the middle isn't.
- **Playful**: pink sparkler light-painting. New in this set, but one step from the Siri waveform.
- **Joy**: Apple keynote gold particles, or a New Year's champagne ad.
- **Calm**: a fluffy white vortex. Anadol-lite, or a hairdryer ad.
- **Awe**: an archery target or sonar ping. It's the galaxy screensaver.
- **Tender** and **fear** are the same glowing orb. Fear is supposed to be a tiny trembling knot in a void.
- **Typing charge** looks identical for every word: grey radial hair, basically the Windows Starfield screensaver. The screen fills up before anything has been judged, and it washes out the black.
- **Return frames** in fear, joy and playful collapse into a glowing *rectangle*. It will read as a bug.

The craft refs ask for matter lit from the centre: rims, glints, depth of field, two-tone dust. The build is still dots and hair. Half of those refs are *eyes*. An iris is the most honest image there is for "a machine that only appraises".

## What people will actually type

`hello` · `penis` · `chatgpt` · `fuck you` · `Sophie` (the ex) · their own name · `❤️` / `💀` · `i love you` · `am i pretty` · `asdfghjkl` · `mama` · `je t'aime` · `死にたい` ("I want to die", half a joke) · `palestine` / `trump` · `skibidi` / `6 7`

About ten of these will land in the calm/tender/joy/playful middle, which currently looks like one glowing centred thing. So `hello`, `mama`, `❤️` and `je t'aime` will feel the same, and people decide it's a mood lamp. The edges are where it pays off. `penis` gets **cut instantly**, no fade, the drone untouched. That's the best idea in the spec, and people will queue to get refused. `死にたい` routing to SUPPORT has to be handled beautifully, because someone will film it. `Sophie` and `asdfghjkl` should make Jev *unsure*, and unsure has to look different from calm. Right now low confidence doesn't show up on screen at all.

## AI demo?

Not in the UI. It is in the logic. Type "anger" and you get the anger preset, which is a mirror, not a judgment. Every word gets the full show. Something that only appraises should sometimes be *unimpressed*.

## Filming, second words, memory, dwell

- **Filmed:** the anger burst, a friend getting barred, and sadness's rain once it stops looking like a curtain.
- **Second and third word:** happen when two close words get noticeably different verdicts (`mum` vs `mother`, `Sophie` vs `Sophie <3`). The drone that remembers is a quiet genius move. Make it audible.
- **The memorable moment:** the refusal, the only time the machine clearly has an opinion *about you*.
- **Dwell:** 2–4 minutes and 3–5 words. The typing is public, and the crowd reading over your shoulder is the social engine. Without more contrast between words, people leave after word three.

## Screensaver risk

High. Every peak is bright in the middle and symmetrical. The fix isn't more particles. It's more refusal, more asymmetry and more darkness.

## Making it judge you

Give intensity a really wide range. A bland `hello` should get a few grains that barely bother, and being under-reacted-to *is* being judged. Let the ANALYZING hold feel like weighing, longer when Jev is less sure. Low confidence should look wrong: off-centre, hesitant, unresolved.

## Sound, gut takes

**Cool:** Ikeda sine ticks, Alva Noto, Biosphere, Mica Levi's *Under the Skin* strings for fear, dust hiss that follows the grains, a sub you feel in your chest, and the drone *ducking out* for a beat before the reaction so the room holds its breath. The dry key clicks are right.
**Cheesy:** D-pentatonic bell arpeggios for joy (that's a meditation app), wind chimes, an Inception BRAAAM on anger, sparkly harp glisses. Pentatonic sounds harmless, and this should sound like it has an opinion.

## If I were the artist I'd…

1. **Kill the default rings.** Rings belong to one emotion at most (anxiety's wobbling topo lines). Calm, awe and sadness each need their own silhouette.
2. **Make fear small:** a pinprick knot, trembling, the whole screen black.
3. **Cut the typing charge by about 70%.** Keep the black until Enter.
4. **Fix the rectangular return clumps.** Grains should go home along the paths they came out on.
5. **Allow tiny reactions.** Take the intensity floor down close to zero, so indifference is one of the verdicts.
6. **Show low confidence** as asymmetry and off-centre drift, with no resolution, and a hanging sus4 in the audio.
7. **Chase the craft refs:** a lit rim, one diffraction glint where the word was, depth of field, two-tone dust. Try an iris for awe.
8. **Keep the sound off pentatonic.** Noise, sine and sub, at most one pitched event per reaction, and a drone memory you can clearly hear by the third word.
