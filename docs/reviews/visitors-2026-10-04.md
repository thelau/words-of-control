**Round 12 — all four, independently:** the middle convinced (the network's nearest words "the best evidence in the
piece": are you alive → are you human, can you feel; poop → booger, fart, crap), the ending undid it — it ended on
weak, literal or negated answers ("ABSURD 0.01" under a father's death, "NO NOSTALGIA" for kill all humans, FLESH,
METAL) and its three nearest words, matched on that one answer, read as noise (the sea at night → despair, cruelty,
death). σ meant nothing to them. A label smudge in the table view.

**Fixed:** rarity no longer counts a flat 0.00 as beyond every word (ties within 0.04); the ending is the marked
answer that sets the words farthest apart that a person would say — never a category, material or colour, never an
absence, never an echo (my dad died last year → SOMETHING OR SOMEONE GONE; kill all humans → VIOLENCE; the sea at
night → LIQUID; taylor swift → JOY); its three nearest words are the nearest on all the answers (i lost my father, my
mother is dying); ranks instead of σ ("TOP 1%", "violence · higher than 99% of 993 words"); labels on the data never
print over each other and sit on black; the header says the mood without a number.

**Round 13 (two visitors):** the endings now read right, but the scores barely moved — what holds them back is no
longer the reading but the form: the same order of scenes every time (colour is most of the difference between
three words), the ring of small 3D views "pretty dust", the endings still literal now and then (LIQUID), and Jev's
limits (it cannot tell "i cheated on her" from being cheated on; it took "kill all humans" seriously). Open for the
artist: the ring, and how much the structure itself should change with the words.
